About the Program

Structure, game model, and student support.

Overview

Our program integrates technical training, tactical literacy, and physical preparation with an academic schedule. We emphasize communication, leadership, and adaptable roles within a clear game model.

Game Model

  • Build-up in organized shapes with width and depth.
  • Pressing triggers off backward passes and poor touches.
  • Transition moments rehearsed with 3-pass rules and zone targets.
“Clarity creates speed.”

Support Structure

Study halls, travel-aware assignment plans, and coach check-ins align with peak competition windows.
